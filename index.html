The Artisan's Journey

Handcrafted by Heena in Hyderabad

Dream Moon was born from a passion for tactile fiber art and aesthetic fashion jewelry. What began as cozy crochet experiments quickly evolved into a beloved indie brand showcased across premier lifestyle exhibitions in Hyderabad like Aangan and Noor-e-Umaish.

Unlike mass-produced metallic jewellery, our yarn pieces are ultra-lightweight, skin-friendly, and completely customizable. We believe your accessories should feel as gentle as they look striking.
